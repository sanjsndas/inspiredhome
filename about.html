About this journal.

An independent publication for people who care about the spaces they inhabit — written by architects, designers, and journalists who believe design writing should be useful, not merely aspirational.

Inspired Home Details is an independent editorial journal focused on residential architecture and interior design. We exist for a specific reason: most design writing online is either thinly veiled advertising or superficial trend reporting. Neither helps anyone make better decisions about the spaces they live in.

Our editorial team is composed of practicing architects, interior designers, and design journalists who write about the subjects they encounter in their work. We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and material knowledge — the kind of information that takes time to acquire and is rarely written down.

A well-planned room is not the result of a large budget. It is the result of careful thought about light, circulation, and material.

Editorial Independence

We do not accept sponsored content, paid placements, or affiliate commissions. When we mention products or materials, it is because they are relevant to the subject at hand, not because someone paid for the mention. Our only revenue comes from reader support and the occasional freelance commission, both of which are kept strictly separate from editorial.

“Design writing should be useful. It should tell you something you can act on, not just something you can admire.” — Editorial statement, first issue

This independence allows us to write honestly about subjects that are often clouded by commercial interests: the real performance of building materials, the trade-offs between cost and durability, the difference between a trend and a principle.

Who We Write For

Our readers include homeowners planning a renovation, architects looking for a well-argued position on a design question, interior designers interested in how their peers approach problems, and anyone who wants to understand why some spaces feel right and others don't.

If you care about the built environment at the domestic scale — the way light falls across a wall, the distance between a sofa and a coffee table, the way a material ages — this journal is for you.

Material honesty: detailing that allows natural finishes to age with grace rather than disguise it.

What We Publish

We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and material knowledge. Our focus areas include:

Interior Design — the planning and composition of interior spaces, from layout to lighting to the selection of finishes and furniture.

Architecture — residential architecture at the scale of the house, including facade design, spatial organization, and the relationship between building and site.

Space Planning — the invisible systems of circulation, sightlines, and zoning that determine whether a floor plan works in daily life.

Materials — the properties, performance, and aging characteristics of the materials used in residential construction and finishes.

Lighting — both daylighting and artificial illumination, explained practically for designers and homeowners.

Home Design — broader considerations of domestic life, from kitchen ergonomics to small-space strategies.

Editorial Standards

Every article we publish is reviewed by at least one member of our editorial team with professional experience in the subject area. We fact-check technical claims, cite sources where appropriate, and correct errors promptly when they are brought to our attention.

We do not publish content generated by AI without human review and attribution. We do not accept guest posts or link insertions. We do not participate in link-exchange schemes or content syndication arrangements that compromise editorial independence.

If you have a correction, a question, or a subject you think we should cover, we welcome your correspondence. Contact us here.

What We Stand For

Usefulness Over Aspiration

We publish information you can act on. A beautiful photograph is not enough. Every article should leave you with a clearer understanding of how to make a better decision about your home.

Independence Over Access

We do not accept sponsored content or paid placements. Our only obligation is to our readers. When we mention a product or material, it is because it is relevant, not because someone paid for the mention.

Depth Over Speed

We publish long-form articles because good design writing takes space to develop an argument. We do not chase trends or publish for the sake of publishing. Every article earns its place.

Material Honesty

We believe in specifying materials that age with grace rather than disguise it. This principle extends to our editorial practice: we are honest about what we know and what we don't.

Practical Knowledge

Our writers are practitioners — architects, designers, and builders. We write about what we know from experience, not what we have read in press releases.

Long-Term Thinking

We write about design that endures, not design that trends. The principles we cover — proportion, light, material, circulation — are the same principles that have governed good building for centuries.

The Weekly Detail

One carefully considered article, one practical design insight, delivered every Sunday. No promotions, no clickbait, no noise.
