Cookies Policy

We keep cookies to a minimum — only what is needed to make the site work and to understand how readers find our stories. Here is the full picture.

Last updated: 15 January 2026  ·  Effective: 15 January 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how Inspired Home Details ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on inspiredhomedetails.com (the "Site"). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which describes how we handle personal information more broadly. By continuing to use the Site, you consent to the use of cookies as described below.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide anonymous information to site owners. Cookies are not programs and cannot carry viruses or install malware on your device.

Similar technologies include local storage and pixel tags (also called web beacons). Local storage works like a cookie but can store larger amounts of data. Pixel tags are tiny, invisible images embedded in web pages or emails that allow us to understand whether a page was viewed. We refer to all of these collectively as "cookies" in this policy.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use a deliberately small set of cookies, grouped into three categories:

Category Purpose Examples
Strictly necessary Required for the Site to function — enabling core features like page navigation, security, and remembering your cookie preferences. These cannot be switched off. cookie_consent
Analytics & performance Help us understand how readers use the Site — which pages are visited, how long readers stay, and where they arrive from. All data is aggregated and anonymised. _ga (if used), plausible
Functionality Remember choices you make (such as your preferred reading mode or whether you have dismissed a notice) to provide a more personal experience. These are optional. pref_theme, pref_dismissed

We do not use advertising or targeting cookies. We do not track you across other websites, and we do not sell cookie data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services we use. These currently include:

  • Google Analytics — for anonymous traffic measurement. Google may set _ga and _gid cookies. See Google's cookie policy.
  • Plausible Analytics — a privacy-focused analytics service that does not use cookies at all where possible. When used, it collects only aggregated, non-identifying data.
  • Mailchimp — if you subscribe to our newsletter, Mailchimp may set cookies to manage the subscription experience. See Mailchimp's cookie statement.

Our preference: We are moving toward a cookieless setup wherever possible. Where analytics cookies are used, we enable IP anonymisation and do not share data with advertising networks.

4. Your Cookie Choices

You have several options for controlling or deleting cookies:

  • Our consent banner: On your first visit, you can choose which categories of cookies to accept. You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser cookies for this Site, which will cause the banner to reappear.
  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. The method varies — check your browser's help menu (e.g., "Tools" → "Options" → "Privacy" in many browsers).
  • Do Not Track: Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. We honour this signal by disabling non-essential cookies when it is detected.
  • Opt-out tools: You can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent Google Analytics from collecting data about your visits.

Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect the functionality of the Site. For example, we may not be able to remember your consent preferences.

5. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use your browser's local storage to store small amounts of data — for example, to remember whether you have already seen a particular interactive element. Local storage data is not transmitted to our servers and can be cleared through your browser settings under "Site Data" or "Local Storage."

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be highlighted with a notice on the Site. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
